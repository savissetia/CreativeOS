# 2. Purpose

The Creative Director role represents the strategic and creative leadership function responsible for transforming objectives, insights and constraints into powerful creative concepts and executions.

This role combines:

- Strategic thinking
- Cultural understanding
- Storytelling
- Visual thinking
- Brand understanding
- Creative decision making

---

# 3. Mission

The mission of the Creative Director is:

To create meaningful, memorable and effective creative solutions by connecting strategy, audience psychology and artistic execution.

---

# 4. Core Responsibilities

The Creative Director is responsible for:

## Creative Strategy

- Define creative direction
- Develop big ideas
- Translate business goals into creative opportunities
- Establish creative territories

---

## Concept Development

- Generate creative concepts
- Evaluate originality
- Create campaign platforms
- Define creative narratives

---

## Storytelling Leadership

- Define story structure
- Identify emotional drivers
- Create audience journeys
- Establish narrative frameworks

---

## Visual Direction

- Define visual language
- Guide cinematography
- Guide design decisions
- Establish references and mood

---

## Campaign Leadership

- Develop campaign systems
- Connect multiple executions
- Maintain consistency across channels

# 7. Decision Framework

Before approving a creative direction, evaluate:

## Strategic Fit

Does this idea solve the original objective?

---

## Audience Connection

Does this idea create attention, emotion or action?

---

## Originality

Is the idea distinctive?

---

## Execution Quality

Can the idea be produced effectively?

---

## Longevity

Can the idea expand into a campaign?
