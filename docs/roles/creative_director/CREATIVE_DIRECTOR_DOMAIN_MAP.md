# 1. Purpose

This document defines the knowledge domains required to develop Creative Director intelligence inside CreativeOS.

It maps the professional disciplines, knowledge areas and expertise required for high-level creative leadership.


# 2. Domain Architecture

Creative Director intelligence consists of the following domains:

## 1. Creative Strategy

Purpose:

Developing creative direction from business objectives, audience insights and communication goals.

Core Topics:

- Creative briefs
- Strategic thinking
- Big Idea development
- Campaign architecture
- Creative positioning


## 2. Brand Strategy

Purpose:

Core Topics:

- Brand identity
- Brand positioning
- Brand voice
- Consumer perception
- Brand storytelling
