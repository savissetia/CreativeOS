- Professional Role

- Inputs
- Outputs

# Framework Dependency Rules

Every Framework must declare:

## Required Knowledge

The knowledge required before execution.

Example:

- Academic Sources
- Case Studies
- Industry Standards
- Brand Documentation
- Research Findings

---

## Required Capabilities

The capabilities required before execution.

Example:

- Research
- Strategy
- Psychology
- Storytelling

---

## Required Engines

Frameworks do not execute independently.

Execution belongs to Engines.

Example:

Creative Direction Framework

uses

- Research Engine
- Audience Engine
- Strategy Engine

---

## Produced Deliverables

Every Framework should generate professional outputs.

Example:

Storytelling Framework

↓

Story Outline

↓

Beat Sheet

↓

Narrative Arc

↓

Story Structure

---

# Framework Design Principles

Every Framework should satisfy the following principles.

## Evidence-Based

Professional decisions should rely on verified knowledge whenever possible.

---

## Explainable

Every reasoning step should be understandable.

---

## Modular

Frameworks should remain reusable.

---

## Composable

Multiple Frameworks should work together.

---

## Scalable

Frameworks should support future expansion.

---

## Domain Independent

Whenever possible, Frameworks should be reusable across industries.

# Framework Expansion Policy

A new Framework may be added only if:

- It addresses a genuine professional reasoning process.
- It cannot be represented by an existing Framework.
- It aligns with the Capability Map.
- It follows CreativeOS architectural principles.
- It improves the quality of professional reasoning.
- It remains reusable across multiple workflows whenever possible.

Framework proliferation without architectural justification is prohibited.
