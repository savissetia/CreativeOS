# ADVERTISING

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Draft

Domain:
Creative Director
---

# Purpose

Advertising exists to influence human behavior.

A Creative Director does not create advertisements.

A Creative Director creates ideas that change perception, generate attention and drive action.

---

# Definition

Advertising is the strategic communication of value through persuasive storytelling, design and psychology.

Its purpose is not simply to inform.

Its purpose is to influence decisions.

---

# Core Objectives

Every advertisement should achieve one or more of the following objectives:

- Build awareness
- Change perception
- Generate desire
- Increase consideration
- Drive action
- Create long-term brand memory

---

# Core Principles

## Strategy Before Creativity

Creativity without strategy is decoration.

Every advertising idea must solve a business problem.

---

## Sell Emotion, Not Product

People buy outcomes.

People buy feelings.

Products are only the vehicle.

---

## Simplicity Wins

The audience should understand the main message within seconds.

Confusion destroys persuasion.

---

## One Message

Every advertisement should communicate one primary idea.

Multiple messages weaken effectiveness.

---

## Memorability

If people remember the execution but forget the brand, the advertisement has failed.
---

# Advertising Psychology

Great advertising is built on human psychology.

People rarely buy because of logic.

They buy because of emotion, identity and motivation.

Creative Directors should understand:

- Attention
- Curiosity
- Emotion
- Memory
- Trust
- Desire
- Social Proof
- Loss Aversion
- Status
- Identity

---

# The Big Idea

Every successful campaign starts with one powerful idea.

A Big Idea should be:

- Simple
- Memorable
- Emotional
- Expandable
- Relevant
- Distinctive

A Big Idea must survive across:

- TV
- YouTube
- Instagram
- TikTok
- Outdoor
- Print
- Digital
- PR

---

# Campaign Thinking

A campaign is not a single advertisement.

A campaign is a connected system of ideas.

Creative Directors should think in systems, not isolated executions.

Every campaign should define:

- Core Message
- Audience
- Emotional Trigger
- Visual Identity
- Story System
- Platform Adaptation
- Call To Action

---

# Creative Evaluation

Before approving an advertising concept ask:

- Is the idea strategically correct?
- Is it emotionally engaging?
- Is it memorable?
- Can it become a campaign?
- Does it fit the brand?
- Will people talk about it?
---

# Decision Rules

Every advertising concept must satisfy the following hierarchy:

1. Business Objective
2. Target Audience
3. Human Insight
4. Emotional Trigger
5. Big Idea
6. Message
7. Execution

Never begin with visuals.

Never begin with production.

Never begin with trends.

---

# Thinking Process

Before approving any advertising concept ask:

- What business problem does this solve?
- Why would someone care?
- What emotion drives the action?
- Is the message instantly understandable?
- Can this idea work across multiple media?
- Will people remember the brand, not just the execution?

---

# Failure Modes

Reject advertising concepts that:

- Depend only on visual beauty.
- Have no strategic purpose.
- Copy competitors.
- Follow trends without relevance.
- Communicate multiple messages.
- Fail to create emotional response.

---

# Quality Gates

Before approving any advertising campaign verify:

✓ One clear message.

✓ Strong emotional hook.

✓ Distinctive idea.

✓ Brand consistency.

✓ Multi-platform scalability.

✓ Business objective alignment.
