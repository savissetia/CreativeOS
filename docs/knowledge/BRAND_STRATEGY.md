# Brand Strategy

## 1. Purpose

Brand Strategy is the discipline of creating a long-term direction for how a brand is perceived, positioned, and experienced in the market.

Within CreativeOS, Brand Strategy is not treated as creating:

* A logo
* A color palette
* A slogan
* A social media page

These are outputs of branding.

Brand Strategy is the strategic foundation behind them.

The purpose of Brand Strategy is to define:

* Why the brand exists
* Who it serves
* What makes it different
* What emotional relationship it creates
* How it should communicate
* How it should grow

---

A professional brand strategist does not start with:

"What logo should we design?"

The strategist starts with:

"What position should this brand own in the mind of the audience?"

---

# Strategic Thinking Layers

CreativeOS analyzes brands through multiple layers:

---

## Layer 1: Business Understanding

Questions:

* What does the company sell?
* How does it create value?
* What business model does it use?
* What are its growth goals?

A brand strategy must support business objectives.

---

## Layer 2: Audience Understanding

Questions:

* Who is the customer?
* What problem do they have?
* What emotions influence their decisions?
* What expectations do they have?

A brand exists in the customer's mind, not only inside the company.

---

## Layer 3: Market Understanding

Questions:

* Who are the competitors?
* What category does the brand belong to?
* What opportunities exist?
* What spaces are not owned?

---

## Layer 4: Brand Meaning

Questions:

* What should people associate with this brand?
* What emotion should it create?
* What memory should remain?
